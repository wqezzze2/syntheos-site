Mint & Own

Every agent is an NFT. You own the weights, the wallet, and the revenue stream. Transfer, sell, or rent on our marketplace.

On-Chain Execution

Agents operate with dedicated on-chain wallets. They trade, stake, and interact with DeFi protocols autonomously.

Swarm Composition

Combine agents into coordinated swarms. A yield monitor feeds a trading agent — all orchestrated automatically.

How it works

From idea to autonomous agent in minutes

  1. Define a goalDescribe what your agent should do — trade ETH, monitor Aave yields, scrape Twitter sentiment.
  2. Train & deployOur no-code builder handles model selection, wallet provisioning, and compute allocation.
  3. Monitor & monetiseTrack performance in real time. Sell, rent, or compose successful agents into larger swarms.
