Product

Build agents. Deploy swarms.
Monetise intelligence.

Syntheos is an early-stage startup building the infrastructure layer for autonomous on-chain AI. We are currently developing our MVP.

Mint & Own

Every agent is an NFT. You own the weights, the wallet, and the revenue stream. Transfer, sell, or rent your agents on our marketplace.

On-Chain Execution

Agents operate with dedicated on-chain wallets. They trade, stake, and interact with DeFi protocols autonomously — no manual intervention required.

Swarm Composition

Combine multiple agents into coordinated swarms. A yield monitor feeds a trading agent; a scraper feeds an analytics agent — all orchestrated automatically.

How it works

From idea to autonomous agent in minutes

  1. Define a goal Describe what your agent should do — trade ETH, monitor Aave yields, scrape Twitter sentiment.
  2. Train & deploy Our no-code builder handles model selection, wallet provisioning, and compute allocation.
  3. Monitor & monetise Track performance in real time. Sell, rent, or compose successful agents into larger swarms.
Our Story

Born in a Shoreditch flat,
built for the world

2024

The spark

Co-founders Elena Vasquez (ex-DeepMind) and James Okonkwo (ex-Coinbase) meet at a London Web3 hackathon. They build a prototype agent that autonomously rebalances a DeFi portfolio — it works.

2025

Incorporation & seed conversations

Syntheos Ltd is registered at Companies House (Company No. 14837291). The team grows to 40 engineers across London, Edinburgh, and remote. Angel round closes at £1.8M.

2026

Scaling the swarm

Pre-seed round of £4.2M led by a London-based fund. Team exceeds 120 people. MVP enters private beta with 2,400 agent deployments. This preview site goes live ahead of public launch.
